# 🌲 Raylings Explorer & Sidebar

Track your progress across all 14 chapters directly inside VS Code!

### 🧭 Raylings Sidebar Explorer
- Click the **Raylings icon (⚡)** in the Activity Bar to open the exercise explorer.
- Expand chapters to view all 66 exercises with real-time status badges:
  - `✓` **Passed / Completed**
  - `⏳` **Pending / Incomplete**
- Click any exercise in the tree view to open it instantly in the editor.

### 💡 Multi-Tiered Progressive Hints
Stuck on a tricky distributed pattern? You don't need to leave the editor:
- Run `Raylings: Show Exercise Hint` from the Command Palette (`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+P`).
- Choose from progressive hint tiers ranging from subtle conceptual pointers to syntax examples.

[Sync Progress](command:raylings.syncProgress)
